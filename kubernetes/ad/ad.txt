Ad
==

What it does
------------
Returns advertisements for the storefront. Only the website asks
for ads. It is not on the checkout path.

flagd can make ads fail, burn CPU, or trigger extra garbage
collection so you can demo a noisy or broken dependency.


Related services
----------------
Called by: frontend

Asks: flagd

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
