Checkout
========

What it does
------------
Finishes the sale when the shopper places an order. It is the
busiest caller in the shop. The website is the only shop service
that calls checkout.

On place-order it typically:
  1. reads the cart
  2. reads the catalog
  3. converts currency
  4. charges payment
  5. asks shipping (which asks quote)
  6. sends email
  7. publishes an event to Kafka (accounting and fraud-detection
     read that later; checkout does not wait for them)

flagd can affect checkout and the services it calls.


Related services
----------------
Calls:
  cart, productcatalog, currency, payment, shipping, email, kafka, flagd

Called by: frontend

Downstream via Kafka (not HTTP): accounting, frauddetection

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
