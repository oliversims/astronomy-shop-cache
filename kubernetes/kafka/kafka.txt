Kafka
=====

What it does
------------
Kafka is a message queue. Checkout writes an "order placed" event
and continues. It does not wait for accounting or fraud-detection.

Those two services read the same topic later. That is the point:
checkout stays fast; bookkeeping and fraud checks happen aside.

Demo only: one pod, no disk. Restart Kafka and old messages are gone.


Related services
----------------
Writes:  checkout
Reads:   accounting, frauddetection

Accounting and fraud-detection wait until Kafka is up before they start.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
