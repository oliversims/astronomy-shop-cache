Currency
========

What it does
------------
Converts money from one currency to another (for example USD to
EUR). It does not charge cards or store orders. It only does the
math so prices and charges use the right amount.


Related services
----------------
Called by:
  frontend   — show prices in the shopper's currency
  checkout   — charge the correct amount at place-order

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
