Accounting
==========

What it does
------------
Writes completed orders into a ledger. Nothing in the shop calls
this over HTTP. There is no svc.yaml.

Checkout publishes an order event to Kafka. Accounting consumes
that event. Checkout does not wait for accounting to finish.

It waits for Kafka to be up before it starts.


Related services
----------------
Reads: kafka (events written by checkout)

No other service has an HTTP client to accounting.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
