Email
=====

What it does
------------
Sends the "thanks for your order" confirmation after a successful
checkout. Shoppers never call this from the browser. There is no
real mail provider in the demo; it is here so checkout has a
confirmation step to trace.


Related services
----------------
Called by: checkout only.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
