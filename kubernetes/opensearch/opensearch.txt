OpenSearch
==========

What it does
------------
OpenSearch is the cabinet for logs. A log is a line of text from a
process (example: payment: card declined for order abc-123). It is
not a trace (Jaeger) and not a metric (Prometheus).

Shop pods do not send logs here themselves. They send OTLP to
otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector). otelcol forwards logs to this
Service on port 9200, index name "otel".

Single-node demo. HTTP, no login (security plugin off). Data is
on emptyDir — restart the pod and old logs are gone.

There is no public OpenSearch hostname. You read logs in Grafana
(https://grafana.simsoliver.com), datasource OpenSearch, after
07-monitoring installs the plugin and points at this Service:

  http://opentelemetry-demo-opensearch.otel-demo.svc.cluster.local:9200


Related services
----------------
otelcol sends logs to opentelemetry-demo-opensearch:9200.
Grafana (namespace monitoring) reads this Service (FQDN above).

Lives in namespace otel-demo (same as the rest of the shop).


Files
