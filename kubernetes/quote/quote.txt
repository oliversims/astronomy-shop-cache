Quote
=====

What it does
------------
Answers "how much does it cost to ship this package?" Shipping
asks quote; shoppers never call quote themselves. Quote does not
talk to the website or to checkout directly.


Related services
----------------
Called by: shipping only.

Shipping then answers frontend and checkout with that price.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
