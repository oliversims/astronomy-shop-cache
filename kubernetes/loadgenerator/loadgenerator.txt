Load generator
==============

What it does
------------
Locust robots that browse and check out so the shop has traffic
without a real customer. They call frontendproxy (the receptionist),
not the frontend Service directly. That matches how a real user
enters: through the public door.

flagd can make them flood the homepage (loadgeneratorFloodHomepage).


Related services
----------------
Calls: frontendproxy
Asks:  flagd

The Locust web UI is on this Service; frontendproxy can route to it.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
