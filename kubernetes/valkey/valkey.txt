Valkey
======

What it does
------------
Valkey is an in-memory store (Redis-compatible). The cart service
keeps basket contents here: what the customer added, removed, or
is viewing.

If the cart pod dies, the basket can still be in Valkey. This demo
has no disk. If the Valkey pod restarts, the basket is empty.


Related services
----------------
Called by: cart only (VALKEY_ADDR).

Cart waits until Valkey is up before it starts.

Also labeled for otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector). This Deployment
has no OTLP endpoint.
