Recommendation
==============

What it does
------------
Suggests other products ("you may also like"). It reads the
product catalog and returns a short list. It does not own the
catalog or the basket.

flagd can break its cache (recommendationServiceCacheFailure).


Related services
----------------
Calls:
  productcatalog   what exists on the shelf
  flagd            optional cache failure

Called by: frontend

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
