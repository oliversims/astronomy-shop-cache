Shipping
========

What it does
------------
Answers delivery questions: cost and (demo) tracking-style info.
It does not invent the price. It asks quote, then returns that
answer to whoever called.

Shoppers do not call shipping from the internet. The website and
checkout call it inside the cluster.


Related services
----------------
Calls: quote

Called by:
  frontend   shipping estimate in the UI
  checkout   shipping as part of place-order

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
