Payment
=======

What it does
------------
Charges the card when checkout places an order. It does not know
the basket or the catalog; checkout already gathered that.

flagd can fail charges (paymentServiceFailure) or make this
service unreachable (paymentServiceUnreachable).


Related services
----------------
Called by: checkout only

Asks: flagd

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
