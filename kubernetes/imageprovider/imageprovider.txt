Image provider
==============

What it does
------------
Serves product photos. The public site does not talk to this
Service directly. frontendproxy fetches images and returns them
to the browser.

This Deployment does not set FLAGD_HOST. Slow-image demo behavior
is not configured in this folder.


Related services
----------------
Called by: frontendproxy

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
