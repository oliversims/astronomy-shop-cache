Product catalog
===============

What it does
------------
Holds the product list: name, price, description, and IDs. It is
the shelf list the rest of the shop reads. It does not handle the
basket or payment.

A flagd flag (productCatalogFailure) can make this service fail
on purpose so you can watch broken traces.


Related services
----------------
Called by:
  frontend         — browse and product pages
  checkout         — line items at place-order
  recommendation   — what to suggest next

Asks: flagd

CI (.github/workflows/ci.yaml) builds this image, pushes GHCR,
and updates deploy.yaml. Argo CD then rolls it out.

Also sends telemetry to otelcol (OpenTelemetry Collector).
