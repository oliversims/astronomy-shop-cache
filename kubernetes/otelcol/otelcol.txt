OpenTelemetry Collector (otelcol)
=================================

otelcol is short for OpenTelemetry Collector.

What it does
------------
This is the mailbox for telemetry. Shop pods send traces, metrics,
and logs here using OTLP (OpenTelemetry Protocol). Other pods already look up the name
opentelemetry-demo-otelcol. You do not open this from a browser.

Traces, metrics, and logs
-------------------------
Three kinds of notes. They are not the same.

  Trace   one customer's trip through the shop (Place order → cart
          → payment → Kafka). Each step is a span: who, what, when,
          how long, success or error. All steps share one trace id,
          like a tracking number. In Jaeger that shows as a timeline,
          not a paragraph of English. Jaeger is in kubernetes/jaeger/.
          otelcol forwards traces there (OTLP gRPC :4317).

  Metric  a number over time, not one customer.
          Examples: checkout_requests_total = 1840,
          payment_latency_ms = 12. Prometheus stores those; Grafana
          graphs them. You cannot replay "this one order" from a metric.

  Log     a line of text from a process.
          Example: payment: card declined for order abc-123.
          Useful when you already know where to look. It is not the
          full path across services unless the line also has the
          trace id.

Shop pods send the trip (traces), the counters (metrics), and the
diary lines (logs) to otelcol, packaged as OTLP. otelcol forwards
traces to Jaeger, metrics to Prometheus (namespace monitoring),
and logs to OpenSearch. It also prints them in its own logs.


It listens on two ports:
  4317  OTLP gRPC  (most shop services)
  4318  OTLP HTTP  (some services; browser traces via frontendproxy)

This version forwards traces to Jaeger (otlp/jaeger), metrics
to Prometheus (otlphttp/prometheus), and logs to OpenSearch.
All three still go to debug.


Related services
----------------
Almost every Deployment in kubernetes/ sends telemetry here
(OTEL_COLLECTOR_NAME / OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT).

frontendproxy also routes /otlp-http to this Service so the
browser can send traces into the cluster.

Traces out: jaeger (opentelemetry-demo-jaeger-query:4317).
Metrics out: Prometheus in monitoring
  (kube-prometheus-stack-prometheus.monitoring.svc.cluster.local:9090/api/v1/otlp).
Logs out: OpenSearch (opentelemetry-demo-opensearch:9200, index otel).


Files
-----
  cm.yaml      collector config (OTLP in; traces to Jaeger;
               metrics to Prometheus; logs to OpenSearch; debug)
  deploy.yaml  one Collector pod
  svc.yaml     ClusterIP on 4317 and 4318
